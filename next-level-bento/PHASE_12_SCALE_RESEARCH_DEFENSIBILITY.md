# Phase 12 — Scale, Research, and Defensibility

## Mission

Grow BentoFlow from a strong 70-page product into a 200+ page authority and durable SaaS without lowering page quality, fragmenting the product, or publishing search-engine-first clutter.

## Growth portfolio target

Long-term composition:

- 30 specialized tools.
- 80 real template pages.
- 30 examples/case studies.
- 30 learning guides and glossary entries.
- 15 framework implementation guides.
- 10 comparison/alternative pages.
- 5 original research or benchmark reports.

This is a direction, not a quota. Publish fewer when quality evidence is insufficient.

## Quarterly opportunity system

Collect:

- Search Console queries and page performance;
- internal search and zero-result queries;
- tool starts, completions, exports, saves, shares, and repeat usage;
- template searches and remixes;
- support and sales requests;
- community discussion and competitor gaps;
- implementation cost and support risk.

Score candidates from 1–5 on:

- verified demand;
- unique job and differentiation;
- product retention value;
- linkability/original evidence;
- monetization fit;
- engineering cost;
- ongoing maintenance and abuse risk.

Require a written brief and owner before adding a route.

## Expansion tool candidates

Only pursue after research:

- card grid and responsive gallery;
- masonry layout with standards-safe fallbacks;
- team, stats, logo cloud, comparison, FAQ, and timeline sections;
- blog and documentation layouts;
- product detail and ecommerce galleries;
- admin shell and data table;
- social image bento designer;
- email-safe layout generator;
- container-query playground;
- subgrid generator;
- design-system documentation generator.

Every new tool must satisfy the Phase 06 contract.

## Original research moat

Publish evidence competitors can cite:

- annual State of Bento UI report based on a transparent sample;
- responsive bento accessibility benchmark;
- framework export size/quality comparison;
- real-world Grid pattern catalogue;
- mobile source-order study;
- template performance benchmark;
- designer-to-code handoff study.

Document methodology, sample limits, test environment, raw/aggregated data policy, and update dates. Do not disguise marketing opinion as research.

## Programmatic SEO governance

- Route registry remains the only publication path.
- No Cartesian keyword combinations.
- Unique interactive state/evidence required.
- Human editorial review required.
- similarity detection blocks near duplicates.
- framework-specific pages need genuinely framework-specific code and guidance.
- auto-generated glossary pages remain drafts until reviewed.
- pages with no value signal enter improve/merge/noindex/retire review.

## Content lifecycle

Quarterly page audit:

- keep and update;
- improve product evidence;
- merge and redirect;
- noindex while incomplete;
- retire after direct redirect;
- maintain as useful non-indexed support content.

Use clicks/impressions as signals, not the only judgment. A low-volume page can be essential for product onboarding or customer success.

## Internationalization

Do not machine-publish dozens of languages. Begin only when demand and support exist:

- locale-aware route and canonical/hreflang plan;
- native-quality translation/review;
- localized screenshots, examples, currency, and terminology;
- local search-intent research;
- update workflow keeping translations current;
- no indexation of incomplete locale trees.

## Community and brand authority

- open examples and selected schema/export packages where strategically useful;
- transparent changelog and public roadmap themes;
- template creator program;
- tutorials with independent educators;
- layout challenges and showcases;
- issue reporting and reproducible bug fixtures;
- annual research launch and newsletter;
- avoid fabricated testimonials or usage counters.

## Operational scale

- SLOs for editor save, export, share, and API.
- capacity and cost dashboards.
- background job retries and dead-letter handling.
- database backup and restore drills.
- schema and exporter deprecation policy.
- dependency/security update process.
- support documentation and incident communication.
- data retention and privacy reviews.

## Metrics

- weekly successful layouts;
- search clicks and qualified tool starts;
- completion/copy/export/save/share by tool;
- repeat usage and cohort retention;
- template-to-remix rate;
- organic backlinks to tools/research;
- free-to-paid conversion and retention;
- page-family maintenance cost;
- content consolidation outcomes;
- support volume and reliability.

## AI-builder execution prompt

> Build BentoFlow’s quarterly scale system using Search Console, privacy-safe product analytics, internal search, support requests, template behavior, competitive gaps, and engineering cost. Score and document every proposed tool/page, enforce the existing registry and quality gates, block keyword permutations and near duplicates, create a content lifecycle dashboard, and produce an improve/merge/noindex/retire plan. Establish original research pipelines with transparent methodology, localization gates, operational SLOs, backup/restore drills, and ecosystem/community programs. Grow toward 200+ pages only when successful user outcomes, evidence, authority, and maintainability increase with page count.

## Completion gate

- No route is published from keyword volume alone.
- Every expansion has unique functionality/evidence and an owner.
- Weak content is actively consolidated.
- Research is reproducible and appropriately qualified.
- Reliability, privacy, and support capacity grow with usage.
